## Review findings

1. **`/providers` also reached the usage endpoint from a hidden tab (corrected).** The PR stated `/providers` does not reach Anthropic. `providerPage.ts` polls `/providers/view` every 10 s, and its Claude card (`ClaudeProviderFacts.snapshot`, `backends/providerStatus.ts`) re-reads `/v1/usage/quota/all` in-process at most every 10 s. A hidden `/providers` tab caused 4 server-side usage checks in 25 s on both main and the contributor head, and the PR's new DESIGN.md rule ("a page whose poll reaches Anthropic pauses while hidden") would have been untrue on merge.
2. **The new test caught a removed guard but not a disconnected one (corrected).** Three mutants survived it: the 10 s interval no longer calling `pollRefresh`, the `visibilitychange` listener removed, and the 1.5 s updating-card timer bypassing `pollUpdate`. After the correction all 8 mutants fail.
3. Acceptable: a show event can refresh twice within about 1.6 s if the 10 s timer fires just after it (2 of 6 shows). At most once per show; the 30 s usage cache and in-flight de-duplication prevent an extra Anthropic call, and `/profiles` already discards out-of-order answers.
4. Not affected: Meridian Desktop loads only its own `index.html`/`tray.html` and blocks navigation, so it never runs these page scripts. Its main process keeps polling `/v1/usage/quota/all` and `/providers/status` every 10 s for notifications, independent of this PR (recorded as a follow-up).
5. Checked with no defect: the drag, open-popover and sign-in/add guards also apply on the show path; hiding fires no fetch; timers are never cleared, so polling resumes; `prerender` counts as hidden; the header (`/health`, `/profiles/list`) and `/telemetry` do not reach the usage endpoint; SPEC and DESIGN.md wording is accurate.
